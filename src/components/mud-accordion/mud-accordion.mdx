## `mud-accordion`

The container. It owns coordination (`mode`), and it pushes `appearance`, `size`,
`icon-position` and `breakpoint` down onto its items — set those here, not on the
individual rows.

<Controls />

## `mud-accordion-item`

One collapsible row. Slotted as a child of `mud-accordion`; it renders its own
header button and panel region in shadow DOM — it is not usable on its own, which is
why its API is documented here rather than on a page of its own. Rendered examples
live under **Molecules → Accordion Item** in the sidebar.

<ArgTypes of={ItemStories} />

### Panel content and spacing

The panel adds no padding: its slot runs edge to edge, as in the Figma Accordion page
(670:5171), and the content spaces itself. In the design `.accordion-content` pads 12px at the
bottom and each `.service-item` row pads 24px (see **With Panel List**). Plain text sits flush
with the heading; wrap it in a container with the padding you need. The stories and demos add
12px below plain-text panels with `mud-accordion-item::part(panel)`, which you can do too.
