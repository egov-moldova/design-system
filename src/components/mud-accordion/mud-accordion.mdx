## `mud-accordion`

The container. It owns coordination (`mode`), and it pushes `appearance`, `size`,
`icon-position` and `breakpoint` down onto its items — set those here, not on the
individual rows.

    name outside them fails `stencil build` with TS2353. The two blocks read
    different parameter keys (`docs.controls` vs `docs.argTypes`), so doing it
    here also keeps one spelling instead of two. */}
<Controls exclude={['host']} />

## `mud-accordion-item`

One collapsible row. Slotted as a child of `mud-accordion`; it renders its own
header button and panel region in shadow DOM — it is not usable on its own, which is
why its API is documented here rather than on a page of its own. Rendered examples
live under **Molecules → Accordion Item** in the sidebar.
